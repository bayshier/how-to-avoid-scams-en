## #338 — The "Boss" in the Video Tells You to Transfer Money? AI Face-Swaps Have Reached the Finance Office: ¥1.56 Million, ¥1.86 Million, ¥300,000

`AI · Account-takeover tech` `AI new scams` `🔓Privacy leaks` `🎯Targets seniors`

During lunch break, Ms. Zhang, a company accountant in Xi'an, received a friend request from a "new number" of her "leader": the avatar was the leader's photo, and in the video call the face, the voice, and the tone were all identical. "Xiao Zhang, transfer the company's money to another account — I need it urgently." She transferred ¥1.56 million — and only learned when the police knocked on her door that this was the city's first AI face-swap fraud case; police froze the funds in a 9-minute emergency intervention and recovered every yuan. Tianjin and Shanghai have since logged a queue of identical cases.

- 1️⃣ Data collection: your "face" was saved away long ago
Stolen contact lists, cracked cloud photo albums, scraped company websites and short-video accounts — the names, titles, photos, and public speech footage of executives and finance staff are all raw material for the face-swap. The smaller the company and the looser its information hygiene, the easier the target.
- 2️⃣ Deepfakes: face-swap plus voice cloning, generated in one click
AI tools produce uncannily realistic video calls and voice messages. In Tianjin, "Auntie Li" took a video call from her "nephew" crying that he needed to borrow money — seeing the "real person" say it face to face dissolved all her caution, and ¥50,000 went out. In another case, the scammer proactively initiated the video call "to prove his identity."
- 3️⃣ The script is matched precisely to your identity
To an accountant: "an urgent project deposit." To an elderly person: "your nephew is in a cash crunch." To a company manager: "help me forward funds — I've already sent you ¥3 million first" (with a forged transfer screenshot, then "large transfers post with delay" as the excuse). Every line is engineered around your daily assumptions.
- 4️⃣ The urgency squeeze
"This concerns major company interests — execute immediately, no delays." "Don't tell anyone." Urgency plus isolation, so you have no time to verify with the real boss. By the time you reconcile accounts in the work group chat, the money has already changed hands three times through first-tier mule accounts.

> ❌ "It was literally his face on video — how could it be fake?" — It can, and this is currently the most dangerous new fraud aimed at corporate finance departments.
> ⚠️ Voices can't be trusted either: Mr. Chen heard a "friend's" voice asking to borrow ¥20,000 — only afterward did he learn it was AI-synthesized; the friend's account had been stolen long before.
> 🛡️ Give your finance team one hard rule: before any transfer, call the leader back on their usual number or confirm in person. During a video call, ask them to wave a hand in front of the face, press their nose, turn their head — AI face-swaps break down under occlusion and large movements.
> 🛡️ People in sensitive roles and leadership should expose fewer photos, voice clips, and videos on the open internet; never park the company contact directory on a public cloud drive.
> ⚖️ AI face-swap fraud is prosecuted as fraud; especially large amounts can mean ten years or more. Illegally obtained personal information stacks on additional privacy-crime charges, punished consecutively.

🛑 The takeaway: if the money has already moved, call 110 (China's police emergency number) immediately, give the receiving account number, and request an emergency freeze — the Xi'an case recovered the full amount because of a 9-minute freeze. When it comes to deepfake "bosses," speed is everything — and the real rule is: no transfer without call-back verification.

## #229 — Inside the "AI Face-Swap" Black Industry: One Photo Beats Facial Recognition — Your Face May Be Committing Crimes for Someone Else

`AI · Account-takeover tech` `🔓Privacy leaks`

"One static photo is all it takes to generate a dynamic video that passes facial-recognition systems 100% of the time?!"
This is not science fiction — it is today's real AI deepfake black-industry playbook. Criminals use your facial data to register accounts, run commercial arbitrage, even drain bank cards. The consequences defy imagination.

**🔍 How does your "face" leak to the black industry?**

Black-industry teams harvest facial material through four main channels:
🕸️ Mass web-scraping: the selfies you post on WeChat Moments, Douyin (Chinese TikTok), Xiaohongshu, and Weibo are the cheapest face source; recruiting, real-estate, and dating sites are also disaster zones.
🕵️‍♂️ Insider leaks (high-value data): property managers, couriers, food-delivery platforms, financial institutions, and medical-beauty clinics hold complete sets of ID, phone, and face data — insider moles often steal and sell them.
🎁 Offline "gift" traps: street teams hand out small gifts in residential communities, parks, and malls, luring passersby into face scans and showing their ID cards.
📄 Fake hiring / agency scams: posing as business-license agents, e-commerce shop-setup services, or recruiters to trick you into sending high-resolution photos holding your ID card.

> ⚠️ Once the black industry has your "face," what happens?
Criminals load a static photo into one-click liveness generators that produce blinking, head-shaking, mouth-opening videos, then put them to work:
📱 Mass-passing real-name verification: batch-registering phone cards, social accounts, ride-hailing and delivery accounts for scalping and rebate farming.
🎭 AI face/voice-swap fraud: impersonating a company boss to trick finance staff into transfers, or impersonating relatives and children with "car crash, debts owed" stories to move the victim's money.
💳 Financial theft and loan fraud: bypassing payment systems to steal funds, or taking out fake loans and corporate credit lines in your name.
🚨 Smear campaigns and sexual extortion: generating pornographic or fabricated "conflict" videos to defame and blackmail victims (the "sextortion" black industry).
> ⚠️ Selling the tools and "taking apprentices": trafficking face-swap software and training others in it already constitutes the crime of "teaching criminal methods"!

> 🛡️ Privacy protection — remember 3 things
🔒 Post with care: avoid publishing high-resolution, unobstructed front-facing selfies and photos holding your ID; switch off unnecessary face-recognition authorizations.
🙅‍♂️ Refuse the freebies: street scan-for-a-gift offers and any activity that requires face enrollment — refuse, always.
📞 Verify through multiple channels: any "relative borrowing money" or "boss requesting a transfer" — no matter how real the video looks — must be confirmed by phone or in person.

🛑 The takeaway: protect your face and your privacy; give the black industry no opening. In the AI era your face is an asset — and a weapon when it falls into someone else's hands. Lock it down: fewer raw selfies online, zero face scans for strangers, and call-back verification for every money request that arrives wearing a familiar face.
