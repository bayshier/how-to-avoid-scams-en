## #3 — "State-Subsidized Online Loans" Are a Gimmick! Usury, forced disbursement, contact-book bombing... Don't step on this mine

`Online loans · Predatory lending` `Black-industry exposé` `🔓Privacy leaks` `🎯Targets debtors`

Tight on money and in a hurry to borrow? Tempted by online posts advertising "state-subsidized loans" or "free national subsidies to bridge you over"? Beware — this is pure bait wrapped up by illegal black-lending rings. So-called "state-subsidized loans" are nothing but disguised usury and scripted loan scams ("taolu-dai" — predatory loans built on rigged contracts). Touch them once, and the consequences are beyond imagining. 👇

**📌 Exposed: the predatory playbook's most profitable and vicious techniques**

- 1️⃣ Hefty "beheading interest" and disguised usury 💸
The beheading deduction: apply for a ¥8,700 loan and only ¥6,300 ever arrives — a large chunk is carved off before you see a cent!
Sky-high "guarantee fees": borrow ¥10,000, repay ¥7,000-plus at 25 days, and another ¥7,000-plus at 30 days! Every extra yuan is packaged as "guarantee fees / service fees" — an annualized rate approaching 6,000%.
- 2️⃣ Forced disbursement and no way to repay 💥
Sold whether you like it or not: money is force-disbursed without the borrower's consent (e.g., "borrow" ¥2,500, forced to repay ¥3,700 within 7 days).
Manufactured default traps: worse still, some lenders shut off the repayment channel after disbursing, deliberately forcing you into "default" so they can demand astronomical penalty fees.
- 3️⃣ Lending under false identities — and a nearly impossible road to redress ⚖️
Deliberate lending under fake identities or non-compliant platforms means that when you later try to defend your rights through legitimate channels, gathering evidence is extremely difficult.
- 4️⃣ Violent collection and "soft violence" harassment 🛑
Privacy leaks & contact-book bombing (mass-harassing everyone in your phone): they force-read your contact list, then flood relatives and friends with mass insults, doctored photos, threats and intimidation.
Illegal lending & inflated principal: even door-step harassment, pressuring borrowers into signing chains of IOUs — grinding the borrower's normal life to dust.

> 🛑 The takeaway — wake-up summary: online loans are NOT a "state subsidy"! No government subsidy is ever disbursed through black-market or scripted loans. However hard your finances are right now, never touch these illegal loans. Step in once and the debt snowballs, dragging you into a collection nightmare without end. Learn the playbook, hold the line, stay away from illegal online lending, and protect your privacy and your money. 💪

## #114 — Hollowed Out for ¥50,000 Before the Loan Even Arrives: The Fake Loan App "Unfreeze Deposit" Scam

`Online loans · Predatory lending` `💸Financial loss`

Whatever you do when you urgently need cash: don't panic. Black-industry rings set their trap with counterfeit loan apps, and in under ten minutes they can bleed tens of thousands of yuan out of you. 💸

**🔍 Full teardown: how the scammers empty your wallet, step by step**

Step 1: cast a wide net, bait the download 🎣
Scammers mass-produce "reskinned" junk loan apps and sow them across web pop-ups and ads, luring downloads with hooks like "we ignore your credit history — blacklists welcome," "low interest, big amounts," "5-year installments."
Step 2: secretly alter the card number, manufacture an "error" ⚠️
You fill in your details; the system shows "approved" and the funds "arrive" in a virtual wallet. But when you try to withdraw: "Incorrect card number, disbursement failed" — because the scammers secretly altered the card number you entered on their backend!
Step 3: fake "customer service" demands the "unfreeze deposit" 💸
Contact support to fix the card number, and they demand "transfer handling fees / risk-account deposits" of ¥10,000-plus — then drag you into an endless loop of "unfreeze fees" with no bottom.

**🚨 The bitter ending**

Even if you wake up, give up the withdrawal, and walk away — when repayment day comes, a blizzard of collection texts still hammers in, demanding you repay a "loan" that never landed in your account. 📉
The greatest danger is not needing money — it's blindly believing in "shortcuts." When your anxiety has been pre-computed, the "hope" you're shown is nothing but a trap tailored to your exact measurements. Always do your borrowing through legitimate, licensed financial institutions. 🛡️

🛑 The takeaway: a loan platform that "approves" you instantly, then charges fee after fee to release money you never received, is 100% fraud. Real lenders don't take deposits to unfreeze your own loan. Scams are everywhere and scammers never run out — follow anti-fraud guidance and stay covered. 👇
