## #24 — Harvesting ¥500,000 a Month? One Computer + AI Automation: Inside the "AI Girlfriend Pay-for-Me" Scam

`Romance · Sexual traps` `💸Financial loss` `🎯Targets women`

Nearly ¥500,000 a month, luxury cars and villas within half a year? Behind this seemingly effortless fortune sits an "affection harvesting" scam that runs on a single computer. Scammers use AI tools and a fake storefront mall to turn thousands of love-starved male fans into ATMs. Every step of the pipeline is a dug pit — reading it will send a chill down your spine.

**🎭 Teardown: how a zero-cost "AI online romance" empties your wallet**

1. Low-cost precision traffic: mass fan-farming 🎣
Black-industry teams mass-post beautiful-woman videos and high-glamour photo sets on short-video and social platforms — content that naturally attracts enormous male followings. Fans are then funneled into private channels (WeChat, Telegram, etc.), corralled in bulk and "cultivated" over time.
2. AI-automated fan-keeping: zero-cost flirtation at scale 🤖
No real person ever appears! The scammer preps a beauty avatar, a complete set of lifestyle photos for the social feed, and carefully designed flirtatious scripts. Relying on AI tools to auto-reply to messages, one operator can simultaneously "date" hundreds of fans at nearly zero operating cost!
3. Fake pay-for-me links / self-built mall: pure sleight of hand 💸
Once a fan is deep in the "tender countryside," convinced he's in a hot romance with the girl of his dreams, the harvest begins:
Forged pay-for-me links: the "girl" acts cute — "Big brother, I saw a dress I love, can you pay for it for me?" — and fires over a payment link for anything from tens to hundreds of yuan. You think it's a normal online checkout; it's actually a fake page the scammer built himself, with no real product and no real shipping!
A self-built mall that lands instantly: the more cunning teams build a dedicated fake mall, so the money shoots straight into the scammer's private account — they skip even the pay-for-me step!
Many victims go on believing, right to the end, that they were paying for love — unaware they were merely one line of "pending transaction volume" in someone's AI system.

**📊 Why the returns grow exponentially**

Once the model is running, the chatting, the cuteness, and the link-sending all convert into revenue automatically. Multiple accounts operating in parallel, funds flowing in without pause — that is the real story behind the black-industry legend of "cashing out for a sports car in six months."

> ⚖️ ⚠️ Legal red line: this is not some "low-cost startup project" — it is fraud with the purpose of illegally taking others' property. Platforms hit these accounts with maximum force; once confirmed, the penalties are a permanent account ban and serious criminal liability.
> 🛡️ Anti-scam reminders: an online "girlfriend" who involves transfers, pay-for-me requests, or shopping links is a scam 100% of the time! Never open a non-official-platform "pay-for-me" link or unknown storefront sent by a network "girlfriend"!

🛑 The takeaway: stay clear-headed and never volunteer to be the ATM for a "girlfriend" you have never met. Any romance that turns into a payment link is a scripted harvest — block it, report it, and keep your wallet shut. 👇

## #210 — No Thugs, No Threats: Inside "Bad-Bunny 3.0" (the Xianrentiao Honey Trap) — From a Date to a Criminal Case

`Romance · Sexual traps` `⛓️Criminal-liability risk` `🎯Targets women`

Do you know how far the honey-trap extortion known in China as "xianrentiao" (lit. "immortal jump" — a woman lures a man into a compromising situation, then accomplices or fabricated evidence extract money) has evolved?
The old version was "you meet your online date at a hotel, then a gang of muscle bursts in, beats you, and takes your cash." That crude, overtly violent extortion is obsolete. The new "Bad-Bunny 3.0" is far more sinister — no gang, no violence; one woman plus one fabricated evidence chain can push you into the depths of a criminal investigation. 🚨

**🕵️ Deep teardown: the fatal three steps of "Bad-Bunny 3.0"**

- 1️⃣ Step one: the long-game disguise, dismantling your guard 🌸
The operator doesn't rush toward a hotel. First comes long, warm, ambiguous chatting online, posing as a single woman looking for true love. Then a normal dinner, a walk — gradual, ordinary escalation, until you believe you've met someone real and drop all suspicion.
- 2️⃣ Step two: covert evidence-gathering, forging an "assault evidence chain" 📸
During the meet-up, the trap is carefully engineered:
Manufactured injuries: at moments of physical contact, she deliberately scratches light marks onto her own body.
Scripted inducement: her words bait you into sounding possessive or domineering — while she secretly records audio and takes photos.
What any normal person would read as couple's banter is later processed into "iron proof" that you harbored intent to assault.
- 3️⃣ Step three: after the date, the mask drops — extortion or criminal accusation ⚖️
The moment it ends, she flips, claiming "I blacked out from drinking that night; I had no capacity to resist," and produces the full kit:
Scratch marks on her body — "proving" violence;
The induced recordings — "proving" premeditation;
Hotel booking and surveillance records — "locking in" the facts.
Then she demands a huge settlement right at the edge of the legal line. If you pay, she takes the money and vanishes — extortion complete. If you refuse, she walks straight to the police and files a rape accusation.

> ⚠️ The most terrifying truth: proving your innocence is brutally hard
Rape is a major criminal case. Once a carefully forged "evidence chain" is submitted, you are pulled into a serious criminal investigation.
Even if investigators eventually clear you and withdraw the case, the experience of being filed against and summoned is nearly impossible to erase — it can inflict irreversible damage on your reputation, your job, and your family life.

> 🛡️ Defense reminders:
🚫 Keep clear boundaries: with opposite-sex strangers online who are overly proactive and accelerate the relationship, stay wary and don't trust easily.
🔒 Keep complete records: on real-world dates, keep your own conduct proper and preserve the full chat context, so nothing can be quoted out of context.
⚖️ Never settle privately: if you're hit with this kind of blackmail, don't pay to protect your reputation — paying only makes the extortionist escalate. Get legal help immediately and tell the police exactly what happened.

🛑 The takeaway: no tenderness falls out of a clear sky. One more ounce of vigilance, one less crisis — keep your wits about you, keep records, and never let a staged date turn into a criminal file with your name on it.
