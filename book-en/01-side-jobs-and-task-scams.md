## #43 · A few taps, several hundred yuan a day? Ms. Wang believed it, paid over ¥1,000, and was blocked on the spot

`Side Jobs & Task Scams` `💸 Money Lost` `🎯 Women` `🎯 Students` `🎯 Job Seekers`

Today we dismantle the "data-entry side job" swindle. You think you're finding a job — you're actually paying the scammer's tuition. Beware. ⚠️

**💥 How the scammers hollow you out, step by step**

Watch how this gray-industry crew operates — every move planned, almost impossible to guard against:

- 1️⃣ Step one: cast a wide net, flood the feeds with tempting ads
"High-paid typing," "document entry," "earn hundreds a day" — the ads are everywhere. Do it from a phone; moms and students welcome. The copy is irresistible: "no experience needed," "set your own hours," "the more you type, the more you earn." It targets people who want to earn from home, and when you scroll past it, the gig seems tailor-made for you. 📱
- 2️⃣ Step two: polished packaging, the "onboarding fee" bait
Once you add the "customer service" contact, they are extremely warm. They ask about your situation, then explain that slots are limited and onboarding requires a "membership fee," "processing fee," or "confidentiality fee." The amounts are small — tens of yuan, maybe a hundred — just low enough to feel acceptable. You reason: a few dozen yuan for a job opportunity, no loss. 💰
- 3️⃣ Step three: ratchet up, exploit the "sunk cost" instinct
One payment, then the next, each with a fresh reason: "processing fee," "confidentiality fee," "deposit," "training fee," "materials fee," "review fee"... You tell yourself you've already paid the earlier ones, so what's a little more. A few hundred, then over a thousand, growing every time. Each payment comes with the assurance that this is "the last one" and tasks will be assigned immediately — but the "last one" is never the last. 💸
- 4️⃣ Step four: the harvest — the final strike
You've paid every fee and expect to start earning. The result? Kicked from the group, blocked, gone. Not a single task was ever assigned. Ms. Wang paid more than ¥1,000 in total, drained her private savings, and got nothing in return but a red exclamation mark. 🚫

**🚨 The cruelest part of the scam: it makes you argue yourself deeper**

Notice: the trick isn't cheating you once — it's making you convince yourself that if you quit now, everything already paid is wasted! The scammer understands "sunk cost" better than you do; the more you can't let go, the deeper you sink.

Why do they target stay-at-home moms and students looking for side work? Fragmented time, a wish to relieve financial pressure, and little knowledge of the tricks. One line — "you can do it from home" — hits with precision. You're thinking about earning money while watching the kids; they're thinking about emptying your pockets.

More vicious still, some scammers give you a taste first:

> ❌ Taste the sweetness, then the slaughter: they let you do a few simple entry tasks and genuinely pay you a few yuan. You see that withdrawal works, and your guard drops. Then they tell you the "high-commission" tasks require a membership fee first... Once you pay, the "high-commission" tasks sit "under review" forever — and then they vanish.
> ❌ Manufactured scarcity and panic: pure theater — first a wall of screenshots showing other people's earnings, then "only two spots left today." The urgency pushes you in.

**🚨 The constant that never changes: every side job that charges you first is a scam!**

Poster-pasting gigs, data-entry gigs, order-brushing gigs ("order-brushing" = fake e-commerce task/rebate scams), like-and-follow gigs, handicraft gigs (stringing beads, sticking labels, packing gift boxes)... different soup, same medicine. One core rule: pay first! However florid the pitch, the moment you're asked for a membership fee, processing fee, confidentiality fee, deposit, or training fee, it is fraud: a fabricated job, fees stacked on fees, then blocked once you've paid everything.

## #147 · From first taste to the tightening noose: one order-brushing scam reels in ¥200,000+ in a single run

`Side Jobs & Task Scams` `💸 Money Lost`

👉 A full teardown of the order-brushing fraud chain — "small rebates + large 'unfreeze' fees + shills in the group." Recognize the psychological warfare, and never sink deeper over "sunk costs"!

Scammer tactics never stop evolving; even completing tasks and withdrawing earnings has become "psychological warfare." One reader's firsthand account: he only wanted pocket money, got strung along from a few hundred US dollars at the start, and in the end lost more than 30,000 USDT (over ¥200,000) — after which customer service and the chat groups vanished into thin air!

His painful experience is laid out below. Pay attention, and never trust this kind of "good gig"! 💸

**🕵️‍♂️ The 4-stage "psychological warfare" of the order-brushing chain**

- 1️⃣ Small sums as bait, trust-building (sweetness first) 📈
At first the victim tried a $100 task on the platform; the "employer" promptly transferred over ¥1,200 to his bank card. Next he tried a $600 task and was paid with five ¥1,000-denomination JD gift cards. Twice burned sweet, he dropped his guard completely.
- 2️⃣ Manufactured anxiety, steered toward big money (deliberate delays) 🪤
After that, on small tasks the platform deliberately made him wait a long time. Customer service stepped in at exactly that moment: "Big tasks mean no waiting and faster withdrawals," luring him into depositing large sums.
- 3️⃣ The chain closes in, the snowball rolls (group shills keep cheering) 💰
After the big task, the platform refused withdrawal with "operation error, data anomaly, unfreeze fee, margin deposit" and demanded ever more money to "top up the task." Shills in the WeChat and Telegram groups played their parts in concert, faking successful withdrawals to sweep away his last doubts.
- 4️⃣ Kicked and blocked, vanished into thin air (squeezed dry, zeroed out instantly) 🚨
When the victim realized something was wrong and demanded a refund, the scammers stopped stalling: kicked from the group, blocked as a contact, evaporated on the spot!

> ⚠️ The iron rules of cutting losses and the safety line
> 🛑 Cut losses on time, refuse to add money: the moment they start citing "unfreeze fees, margin deposits, task top-ups" to make you keep transferring, your original money is already gone! Never sink deeper over "sunk costs"!
> ⚖️ Criminal exposure and fund safety: joining or promoting such offshore crypto / order-brushing platforms not only leaves your money unprotected — it can implicate you in illegal foreign-exchange trading and illegal fundraising.
> 🔒 Prevention iron rule: anything promising "order-brushing rebates, fast large-amount withdrawals, pay an unfreeze fee" is fraud, 100% of the time.

No pie falls from the sky. Protect your wallet; don't let one moment of greed become a lifetime of regret.
Forward this to friends doing side gigs or touching forex / order-brushing platforms — wake them up in time! 👇
